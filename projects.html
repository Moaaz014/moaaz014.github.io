Projects

GitHub profile

Selected work in digital design, embedded systems, and applied sensing.

Most of these projects were developed as part of a team.

RTL-to-GDS digital system

A low-power digital system with a configurable clock structure and UART control. Work covered RTL design, verification, synthesis, formal equivalence, and physical implementation.

Arithmetic unit & memory hierarchy

A structural VHDL arithmetic system with a ROM/RAM datapath, controller logic, and self-checking testbenches.

Non-invasive glucose monitoring

A sensor-data pipeline for non-invasive glucose monitoring, combining acquisition, dimensionality reduction, and machine-learning classification.

Food packaging & quality sensing

A food-quality sensing system for packaging research, combining MQ137 measurements with a Random Forest classification model.

Wearable health monitoring

A wearable monitoring system combining biomedical sensors, a custom PCB, and machine-learning classifiers. This work contributed to the wearable-sensing publications.

TinyML for sleep & activity recognition

A wrist-worn system that collects motion and pulse signals and runs sleep and activity models directly on a microcontroller.
